Chapter 01 · The beginning

Origin of
everything.

Before there were stars, planets or time as we know it, there was an extraordinarily hot, dense beginning. The universe has been expanding and changing ever since.

13.8B

100B+

Chapter 02 · A cosmic timeline

How we
evolved.

From the first particles to a world that looks back at the stars, this is the remarkable chain of events that made our place in the cosmos possible.

13.8 BILLION YEARS AGO

The Big Bang

Space begins expanding. Energy and matter set the stage for everything that follows.

13.6 BILLION YEARS AGO

First stars

Gravity gathers gas into the first stars, lighting up the young universe.

4.6 BILLION YEARS AGO

Our solar system

A cloud of gas and dust forms the Sun, planets and countless smaller bodies.

4.5 BILLION YEARS AGO

Earth takes shape

Our home becomes a living planet — one that will eventually ask where it came from.

Chapter 03 · The human quest

Milestones
crossed.

Curiosity carried us from watching the night sky to reaching beyond our world. These are a few moments that changed how humanity sees the universe.

1609

A closer look

Galileo turns a telescope toward the sky and reveals details never seen before.

1957

First satellite

Sputnik 1 becomes the first artificial satellite to orbit Earth.

1969

One giant leap

Apollo 11 lands humans on the Moon for the first time.

1990

A new window

The Hubble Space Telescope begins observing the universe from orbit.

2015

New worlds

New Horizons sends humanity its first close-up views of Pluto.

2022

Deeper into space

The James Webb Space Telescope releases its first full-colour science images.
